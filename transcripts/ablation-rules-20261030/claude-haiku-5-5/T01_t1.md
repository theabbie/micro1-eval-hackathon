## Reply

**Time-zone handling.** Offset-bearing values are absolute instants. Offset-less values (the query dates) are read in America/New_York, so `2024-12-31` covers `[2024-12-31T05:00Z, 2025-01-01T05:00Z)`. This matters for a1c-02 (`2025-01-01T03:00Z` is still 2024-12-31 22:00 EST) and a1c-12 (`2025-01-01T06:00Z` is 2025-01-01 01:00 EST).

**q1** (`code` in 4548-4 or 17856-6 on loinc; status final/amended/corrected; `date` ge 2024-01-01 and le 2024-12-31)
- a1c-01: match (2024-03-14, final).
- a1c-02: match. Its instant is before 2025-01-01T05:00Z, so `le` holds, and it is after 2024-01-01T05:00Z.
- a1c-03: no. Its instant (2024-01-01T02:00Z) is before the `ge` start (2024-01-01T05:00Z).
- a1c-04: match (17856-6, amended, 2024-07-02).
- a1c-05: no (entered-in-error).
- a1c-06: no (preliminary).
- a1c-07: no (local-lab system, not loinc).
- a1c-08: match (corrected, loinc coding present, 2024-11-20).
- a1c-09: match. The year 2024 interval overlaps both bounds.
- a1c-10: no (code 59261-8).
- a1c-11: no (coding has no system).
- a1c-12: no. Its instant (2025-01-01T06:00Z) is after the `le` end (05:00Z).

**q2** (loinc 4548-4; value > 9 with unitsofmeasure `%`; status final/amended/corrected)
- a1c-02: match (9.4, final).
- a1c-12: match (9.1, final).
- a1c-03: no (9.0 is not > 9).
- a1c-06: no (status preliminary; also 9.6 would otherwise qualify).
- Others fail on value, status, code, or system.

**q3** (`code=4548-4` with any system; `value-quantity=8.0`, implicit range [7.95, 8.05); no status filter)
- a1c-05: match (8.01; status is not filtered).
- a1c-08: match (7.95 is the inclusive lower bound).
- a1c-11: match (8.0; no-system coding still matches a code-only token).
- a1c-07: no (8.05 is excluded by the half-open upper bound).
- Others fall outside the range or have a different code.

**q4** (`code=|4548-4`, no system)
- a1c-11: only resource with a 4548-4 coding that has no system.

**q5** (loinc 4548-4; `date=2024-12-31` as `eq`, interval `[2024-12-31T05:00Z, 2025-01-01T05:00Z)`)
- a1c-02: match. Its minute-precision instant lies wholly inside the local day.
- a1c-09: no. The year-2024 interval is not wholly inside the day.
- a1c-12: no. Its instant is after the day ends.
