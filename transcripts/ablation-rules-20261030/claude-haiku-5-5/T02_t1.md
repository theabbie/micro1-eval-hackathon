**Query results**

- **q1** (`date=2024-11-03`, eq): every instant of T must lie inside the day. k-02 (before the day), k-04 (starts exactly at the end), k-10 (month), and k-11 (Period starts 11-02) fail. The rest match.
- **q2** (`ge` 05:00Z AND `lt` 06:00Z): needs an instant in [05:00Z, 06:00Z). k-05 (05:30Z), k-10 (month overlaps), k-11 (Period 02:00Z to 11:01Z overlaps), and k-12 (exactly 05:00Z) match. k-13 (06:00Z) fails the strict `lt`. k-08 ends at 05:00Z exclusive, so it does not overlap.
- **q3** (`sa` 05:00Z): every instant of T must be at or after 05:00Z. k-08 ends before 05:00Z exclusive, so it fails. The others from 05:00Z onward match.
- **q4** (`eb` 04:00Z): every instant of T must be before 04:00Z. Only k-02 qualifies. k-11 starts at 02:00Z but runs past 04:00Z, so it fails.
- **q5** (`ne`): all resources not inside the day. Resources with a value are never excluded by the prefix, so the result is the complement of q1.
- **q6** (`eq` 06:30Z to 06:31Z): k-06 and k-07 are both 06:30Z. k-13 (06:00Z) is outside.
