## Reply

**Setup:** Local zone is America/Los_Angeles. In January 2020 that is PST (UTC-8). Date-only values are whole local days, and `onset-date` indexes only `onsetDateTime` and `onsetPeriod`. `onsetAge` and `onsetString` are not indexed. Clinical-status codes are matched case-sensitively, and "code alone" matches any system.

**Clinical-status "active" (any system, exact case):** c-01, c-03, c-04, c-05, c-07, c-08. c-10 has code `Active` (capital A), so it does not match.

**Onset intervals:**
- c-01: 2018-03-02 (day)
- c-02: 2019-07-01 (day)
- c-05: period from 2019-10-01 PDT to end of 2020-02, i.e. before 2020-03-01
- c-06: 2015 (year)
- c-07: 2020-01-01T07:30Z = **2019-12-31 23:30 PST**
- c-08: 2020-01-01T09:00Z = **2020-01-01 01:00 PST**
- c-09: 2017-05-05 (day)
- c-10: 2016-08-19 (day)
- c-03 (onsetAge), c-04 (onsetString), c-11 (none): no indexed date value

**q1: clinical-status=active & onset-date=lt2020-01-01**
The search interval starts at 2020-01-01 00:00 PST. A resource matches `lt` if some instant of its onset lies before that.
- c-01: yes (2018).
- c-05: yes (period starts 2019-10-01).
- c-07: yes (23:30 PST on 2019-12-31 is before midnight).
- c-08: no (01:00 PST on 2020-01-01 is not before midnight).
- c-03, c-04: no indexed onset value.

**q2: clinical-status=active,recurrence,relapse & onset-date=ge2020-01-01**
`ge` requires some instant at or after 2020-01-01 00:00 PST, or overlap with the search range.
- c-05: yes (period runs into 2020).
- c-08: yes (01:00 PST on 2020-01-01).
- c-07: no (ends at 23:30:01 PST on 2019-12-31).
- c-02 (recurrence, 2019): no. c-01: no.

**q3: onset-date:missing=true**
Resources with no indexed date-typed onset: c-03 (Age), c-04 (string), c-11 (no onset). c-06, c-09 and c-10 have dates, so they are excluded.

**q4: clinical-status:not=active**
`:not` includes resources with no matching value. That covers c-02 (recurrence), c-06 (resolved), c-11 (inactive), c-09 (no clinicalStatus, so it has no value) and c-10 (`Active` does not match `active`). The six active resources are excluded.

**q5: onset-date=2019**
The search interval is [2019-01-01 00:00 PST, 2020-01-01 00:00 PST). `eq` requires every instant of the resource's onset to fall inside it.
- c-02: yes (2019-07-01).
- c-07: yes (23:30 PST on 2019-12-31 is inside).
- c-05: no (the period extends into 2020).
- c-08: no (01:00 PST on 2020-01-01 is outside).
