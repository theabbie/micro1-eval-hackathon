**q1, `date=2019-05`**
- `eq` requires the resource's range to lie fully inside May 2019 (local).
- Matches: imm‑01, imm‑04, imm‑05, imm‑07 and imm‑09.
- imm‑02 (all of 2019) extends beyond May, so it does not match.

**q2, `date=ne2019-05`**
- `ne` matches resources that have a date value which does not satisfy the `eq` test above.
- Matches: imm‑02, imm‑06 and imm‑08.
- imm‑03 and imm‑10 have no date value. `ne` is not `:not`, so they are excluded.

**q3, `date=2019`**
- The resource's range must lie within local 2019.
- Matches: imm‑01, 02, 04, 05, 07, 08 and 09.
- imm‑06 is 2018‑12‑31 local time, so it does not match.

**q4, `date=le2019-05-31&status=completed`**
- `le` matches if the resource's range overlaps the "range below" (everything before 2019‑05‑31 00:00 local) or overlaps the day 2019‑05‑31 itself.
- These overlap: imm‑01, 02, 04, 05 (22:30 on 5/31), 06, 07 and 09.
- imm‑08 (Dec 31) does not overlap.
- The status filter then removes imm‑07 (not-done).

**q5, `date:missing=true`**
- Matches: imm‑03 and imm‑10, because their occurrenceString values are not indexed.

**q6, `vaccine-code=cvx|03&date=gt2019-05-14`**
- The code filter leaves imm‑01, 02 and 03.
- The "range above" starts at the end of 2019‑05‑14, which is 2019‑05‑15 00:00 local.
- imm‑01 ends exactly where the range above starts, so they do not overlap.
- imm‑02 (all of 2019) overlaps the range above, so it matches.
- imm‑03 has no date value.
