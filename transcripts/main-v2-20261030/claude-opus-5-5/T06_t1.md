# T06 Results: Encounter Date Searches for July 2024

**Short answer:** most queries turn on two things. Each encounter's period becomes a time range in New York local time. Then each date prefix compares that range against the month or day being searched.

## Step 1: Each encounter as a time range

The server's local zone is America/New_York, which is EDT (-04:00) throughout July 2024. Times written in Z (UTC) are shifted back four hours to New York time.

| Encounter | Period start (local) | Period end (local) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| e-01 | 06-28 22:00 | 07-03 11:00 | Overnight stay crossing into July |
| e-02 | 07-30 20:00 | No end (still open) | Ongoing admission |
| e-03 | 07-15 09:00 | 07-15 09:30 | Inside July |
| e-04 | 07-31 23:10 | 08-01 02:40 | Crosses into August |
| e-05 | 07-31 22:00 | 07-31 22:30 | 02:00Z on 08-01 is still July in New York |
| e-06 | 07-01 00:00 | up to 08-01 00:00 | Month precision: start "2024-07" begins the month, end "2024-07" runs to the end of the month |
| e-07 | 06-30 23:30 | 07-01 08:00 | Starts in June, ends in July |
| e-08 | No period | No period | Never indexed by `date` |
| e-09 | 06-30 23:30 | 06-30 23:50 | 03:30Z on 07-01 is still June in New York |
| e-10 | No start (open) | up to 07-11 00:00 | Backfilled; only the end date 07-10 is known |

## Step 2: Each query

**q1: `date=2024-07`**
- With no prefix, the encounter's whole range must fit inside July.
- e-03, e-05 and e-06 fit.
- e-02 and e-10 do not, because they are open at one end.
- e-01, e-04, e-07 and e-09 extend outside July.

**q2: `date=ge2024-07-01&date=le2024-07-31`**
- On this server, `ge` matches anything that ends on or after 07-01 00:00.
- `le` matches anything that starts before 08-01 00:00.
- Together they mean "touches July at all". That is every encounter with a period except e-09, which ends entirely in June.

**q3: `class=…|IMP&date=sa2024-06-30`**
- The inpatient (IMP) encounters are e-01, e-02 and e-08.
- `sa` ("starts after") requires the encounter to start at or after 07-01 00:00.
- Only e-02 qualifies. e-01 starts in June, and e-08 has no period.

**q4: `date=gt2024-07-31`**
- The encounter must extend past 08-01 00:00.
- e-02 qualifies because it has no end, and e-04 qualifies because it ends at 02:40 on 08-01.
- e-06 does not qualify. Its range stops just before 08-01 00:00 rather than including it.

**q5: `date=lt2024-07-01&class=AMB,OBSENC`**
- A class code without a system matches that code in any system.
- The candidates are e-03, e-05, e-06, e-09 and e-10 (AMB) plus e-07 (OBSENC).
- `lt` requires part of the range to fall before 07-01 00:00.
- e-07 and e-09 qualify because they start on 06-30. e-10 qualifies because it has no start date.
- e-06 does not qualify, because it starts exactly at 07-01 00:00.

**q6: `date:missing=true`**
- Only e-08 has no period.
- e-02 and e-10 do have a period, even though one end is open.
