**Search ranges.**
- February: [Feb 1 00:00 EST, Mar 1 00:00 EST)
- March: [Mar 1 00:00 EST, Apr 1 00:00 EDT)
- 2024-03-01: [Mar 1 00:00 EST, Mar 2 00:00 EST)
- 2024-03-31: [Mar 31 00:00 EDT, Apr 1 00:00 EDT)
- gt 2024-03-31T23:59-04:00: the range above starts at Apr 1 03:59:59.999Z

**Prefix semantics.** I applied the R4 table with the server's overrides:
- eq and ne: the search range fully contains the target (eq), or it does not (ne).
- ge and le: also match on any overlap.
- gt and lt: the range above or below must intersect the target.
- sa and eb: the target must not overlap the search range and must lie entirely above or below it.

## Per-query results

- **q1 (eq 2024-02):** Targets must lie fully inside February. g-09 (spans into March) and g-15 (whole year) do not qualify. g-11 starts exactly at the March boundary and is excluded.
- **q2 (eq 2024-03):** Targets must lie fully inside March. g-09, g-15 and g-16 do not qualify. g-04 (00:30 EDT on Apr 1) is excluded.
- **q3 (ge 2024-03-01 AND le 2024-03-31):** ge requires the target to end at or after Mar 1 00:00 EST. le requires it to start before Apr 1 00:00 EDT. Overlap is enough, so g-09, g-15 and g-16 are added relative to q2. g-05 and g-12 fail ge. g-04 fails le.
- **q4 (eb 2024-03-01):** Targets must end before Mar 1 00:00 EST and not overlap. g-11 ends exactly at the boundary and is excluded.
- **q5 (sa 2024-03-31):** Targets must start after Apr 1 00:00 EDT. Only g-04 qualifies.
- **q6 (ne 2024-03):** Excludes only targets fully inside March (g-02, g-03, g-06, g-07, g-08, g-11, g-14). Non-overlapping and partially overlapping targets remain.
- **q7 (gt 2024-03-31T23:59-04:00):** Targets must extend past Apr 1 03:59:59.999Z. g-03 ends at 03:30Z and is excluded. g-16 is open-ended, so it qualifies.

**Assumption:** a period with only a start is treated as open-ended (ongoing). If the server instead treated a missing end as empty, g-16 would drop out of q3, q6 and q7.
