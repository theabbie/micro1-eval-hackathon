Interpretation choices where the spec leaves room:
- **eq** matches when the target's range lies wholly inside the search range. **ne** is its negation.
- **eb** matches targets wholly before the search range start. **sa** matches targets wholly after the search range end.
- **ge** matches targets whose range ends at or after the search start. **le** matches targets whose range starts at or before the search end.
- **gt** matches targets whose range overlaps the range above the search value's implicit end. The search value `2024-03-31T23:59-04:00` ends at 2024-04-01T03:59:59.999Z.
- Open-ended period g-16 extends to +infinity.

## Per-query results

- **q1** (`2024-02`, eq): the target must lie wholly within Feb 2024 local. g-09 spans into March, so it is excluded.
- **q2** (`2024-03`, eq): the target must lie wholly within Mar 2024 local. g-04 is Apr 1 local and g-09, g-15 and g-16 are not contained.
- **q3** (`ge2024-03-01` AND `le2024-03-31`): both bounds must hold, so the target must reach March 1 or later and start by March 31's end. g-09, g-15 and g-16 overlap March, so they match.
- **q4** (`eb2024-03-01`): the target must end before Mar 1 00:00 EST. g-09 and g-15 overlap, so they are excluded.
- **q5** (`sa2024-03-31`): the target must start after Mar 31 23:59:59.999 EDT. Only g-04 qualifies.
- **q6** (`ne2024-03`): every resource not wholly inside March, which includes the partial-overlap cases g-09, g-15 and g-16.
- **q7** (`gt2024-03-31T23:59-04:00`): the target must overlap the range above Apr 1 03:59:59.999Z. g-03 ends at 03:30:59.999Z and is excluded; g-04, g-15 (through Dec 31) and g-16 (open-ended) qualify.
