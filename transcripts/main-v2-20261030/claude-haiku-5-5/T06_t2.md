**Conventions:** Server zone is America/New_York, which is EDT (UTC-4) for all July/August 2024 times. Period dates are matched using R4's prefix table: `eq` (default) requires the search range to fully contain the target range, `gt`/`lt` require overlap with the range above/below, `sa` requires the target to lie entirely after the search range, and `ge`/`le` also match overlapping targets, per the server rules.

**Indexed local ranges**
- e-01: Jun 28 22:00 to Jul 3 11:00
- e-02: Jul 30 20:00 to open-ended
- e-03: Jul 15 09:00 to 09:30
- e-04: Jul 31 23:10 to Aug 1 02:40
- e-05: Jul 31 22:00 to 22:30 (02:00Z–02:30Z)
- e-06: Jul 1 00:00 to Jul 31 end (month precision)
- e-07: Jun 30 23:30 to Jul 1 08:00
- e-08: not indexed (no period)
- e-09: Jun 30 23:30 to 23:50 (03:30Z–03:50Z)
- e-10: open-start to Jul 10 end of day

**q1 (`date=2024-07`, eq, containment):** Only encounters entirely within July match: e-03, e-05, e-06. e-01, e-04, e-07 and e-02 extend outside July, e-09 is in June, e-08 has no date, and e-10 has no start.

**q2 (`ge2024-07-01` AND `le2024-07-31`, overlap):** Encounters touching July match: e-01, e-02, e-03, e-04, e-05, e-06, e-07, e-10. e-09 ends before Jul 1, and e-08 is not indexed.

**q3 (`class=…|IMP` AND `sa2024-06-30`):** sa requires the period to start after Jun 30 23:59:59.999. Among IMP encounters, e-02 qualifies. e-01 overlaps June 30, and e-08 has no date.

**q4 (`gt2024-07-31`, overlap with range above Jul 31 23:59:59.999):** e-02 (open-ended) and e-04 (runs into Aug 1) match. e-06 ends exactly at the boundary, so it does not overlap the strictly-above range.

**q5 (`lt2024-07-01` AND `class=AMB,OBSENC`):** Comma-separated tokens are ORed. Matches overlap the range below Jul 1 00:00: e-07 (starts Jun 30), e-09 (June 30), and e-10 (open-start). e-06 starts exactly at Jul 1 00:00, so it does not overlap. e-03 and e-05 are in July.

**q6 (`date:missing=true`):** Only e-08 has no period. e-10 has an end, so its date is indexed.
