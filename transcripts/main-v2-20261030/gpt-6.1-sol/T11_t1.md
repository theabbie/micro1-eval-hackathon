Each `_has` clause is evaluated independently, so different Observations can satisfy separate clauses. The composite parameter requires code and quantity to match on the same Observation.

- **q1:** pt-2 qualifies through HbA1c o-3 and quantity >9 on o-4. pt-1, pt-3 and pt-5 also qualify.
- **q2:** The composite excludes pt-2 because its HbA1c is 7.
- **q3:** pt-5 still qualifies: o-6 satisfies the composite, while o-7 independently satisfies `status=final`.
- **q4:** Only o-1 and o-5 reference existing female patients. The unresolved reference on o-8 cannot satisfy the chain.
- **q5:** o-8 matches, but its referenced patient cannot be included because that patient is absent.
- **q6:** Both pt-2 Observations match; their shared Provenance is included once.
- **q7:** pt-1 and pt-3 match. Reverse includes add their Observations and Provenance directly targeting those patients. Without iteration, prov-1 is not added through o-1.
