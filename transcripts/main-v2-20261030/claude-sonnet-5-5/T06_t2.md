I treated a missing `period.start` as open at the start and a missing `period.end` as open at the end. A Period with one bound is still indexed.

**Key conversions**
- e-05 is Jul 31 22:00–22:30 EDT, so it falls inside July.
- e-09 is Jun 30 23:30–23:50 EDT, so it falls just before July.
- e-07 runs Jun 30 23:30 to Jul 1 08:00 EDT, so it overlaps July.

**q1 `date=2024-07`** (eq: the target must be fully contained in July)
- Match: e-03, e-05, and e-06 (both bounds are month-precision July).
- Not contained:
  - e-01, e-04, e-07 and e-09 cross a boundary.
  - e-02 is open-ended.
  - e-10 has no start.
  - e-08 has no period.

**q2 `date=ge2024-07-01&date=le2024-07-31`** (AND of two conditions)
- `ge` needs the target end to be at or after Jul 1 00:00, or open-ended.
- `le` needs the target start to be before Aug 1 00:00, or missing.
- Match: e-01, e-02, e-03, e-04, e-05, e-06, e-07, and e-10 (missing start passes `le`, and its end of Jul 10 passes `ge`).
- Fail: e-09 ends before Jul 1, and e-08 has no period.

**q3 `class=…|IMP&date=sa2024-06-30`**
- IMP encounters are e-01, e-02, and e-08.
- `sa` needs the target start to be after the end of Jun 30, which is Jul 1 00:00.
- Only e-02 (starts Jul 30) qualifies. e-01 starts Jun 28, and e-08 has no period.

**q4 `date=gt2024-07-31`**
- The target must extend past Aug 1 00:00, or be open-ended.
- Match: e-02 (open-ended) and e-04 (ends Aug 1 02:40 EDT).
- e-05 ends Jul 31 22:30 EDT, and e-06 ends at the end of July, so neither qualifies.

**q5 `date=lt2024-07-01&class=AMB,OBSENC`**
- The `class` token has no system, so it matches on code alone.
- `lt` needs the target start to be before Jul 1 00:00, or missing.
- Candidates are the AMB and OBSENC encounters: e-03, e-05, e-06, e-07, e-09, e-10.
- Match: e-07, e-09, and e-10 (no start).
- e-06 starts at exactly Jul 1 00:00, so it is not below.

**q6 `date:missing=true`**
- Only e-08 has no period. e-10 has an end, so it is indexed.
