**q1: eq on the day.** A match needs the target's range to be fully contained in the search day.
- k-02 is before the day (Nov 2 23:30 EDT), so it fails.
- k-04 is at 00:00 EST on Nov 4, so it fails.
- k-10 (month) and k-11 (period starting before the day) are not contained, so they fail.
- k-08 equals the day exactly, so it matches.
- Everything else falls inside the day.

**q2: ge 05:00Z AND lt 06:00Z.** Here ge matches a target whose range intersects [05:00Z, ∞), and lt matches a target whose range intersects (−∞, 06:00Z).
- k-05 (05:30Z) matches.
- k-12 (05:00Z) matches.
- k-08 (whole day) matches.
- k-10 (month) matches.
- k-11 (02:00Z to 11:00Z) matches.
- k-13 (06:00Z) fails lt, because the range below ends at 06:00Z.
- k-06 and k-07 (06:30Z) fail lt.
- k-01 (04:30Z) fails ge.
- k-03 and k-09 (about 04:30Z to 04:45Z on Nov 4) fail lt.

**q3: sa.** The target must start at or after the end of the day, which is Nov 4 05:00Z. Only k-04 qualifies.

**q4: eb.** The target must end before 04:00Z on Nov 3. Only k-02 (03:30Z) qualifies.

**q5: ne.** This is the complement of q1 among resources that have a date value: k-02, k-04, k-10 and k-11.

**q6: eq 2024-11-03T01:30-05:00.** The search range is [06:30Z, 06:31Z). The matches are k-06 and k-07, both at exactly 06:30Z. k-05 is at 05:30Z, and k-08 is not contained in the range.
