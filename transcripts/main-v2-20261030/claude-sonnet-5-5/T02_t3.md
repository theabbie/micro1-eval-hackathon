**q1 (eq, date 2024-11-03)**
- A target matches only if its whole range lies inside the search day.
- k-08 matches because its range equals the day.
- k-10 (month) and k-11 (period) stick out of the day, so they do not match.
- k-02 and k-04 fall outside the day.
- All remaining instants fall inside it.

**q2 (ge 01:00-04:00 and lt 01:00-05:00)**
- The two parameters are ANDed.
- `ge 01:00-04:00` has a minute-precision range starting at 05:00Z. It matches targets that overlap [05:00Z, ∞).
- `lt 01:00-05:00` has a range starting at 06:00Z. It matches targets that overlap (−∞, 06:00Z).
- k-05 (05:30Z) and k-12 (05:00Z) satisfy both.
- k-13 (06:00Z) fails `lt` because it is not below 06:00Z.
- k-08, k-10 and k-11 are ranges that overlap both bounds, so they match.
- k-03 and k-09 are on 11-04, so they fail `lt`.
- k-06 and k-07 (06:30Z) fail `lt`.
- k-01 and k-02 end before 05:00Z, so they fail `ge`.

**q3 (sa)**
- A target must start at or after 11-04 05:00Z. Only k-04 does.
- k-03 is 04:30Z, which is too early.

**q4 (eb)**
- A target must end at or before 04:00Z on 11-03. Only k-02 does.
- k-01 starts at 04:30Z, so it is not before.

**q5 (ne)**
- ne is the complement of q1: k-02, k-04, k-10 and k-11.

**q6 (eq 01:30-05:00)**
- The search range is the minute from 06:30Z to 06:31Z.
- k-06 and k-07 are both exactly 06:30:00Z.
- k-05 is 05:30Z, which is a different instant.
