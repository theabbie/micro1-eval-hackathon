## Reply

**Key rules applied**

- Offset-less dates are interpreted in America/Los_Angeles (PST, UTC−8 in January 2020).
- `2020-01-01T07:30:00Z` is 2019-12-31 23:30 PST, and `2020-01-01T09:00:00Z` is 2020-01-01 01:00 PST.
- Only dateTime, date, instant, and Period values are indexed by `onset-date`. `onsetAge` and `onsetString` are not indexed.
- Token matching is case-sensitive and ignores the system unless one is given. The local-system `"Active"` in c-10 does not match `active`.
- For `lt`, the range below ends at the start of the search range. For `ge`, the match is any target whose range ends on or after the start of the search range.

**q1: `clinical-status=active&onset-date=lt2020-01-01`**
- Active resources: c-01, c-03, c-04, c-05, c-07, c-08. c-10 (`Active`, local system) does not match, and c-09 has no clinicalStatus.
- c-01 (2018-03-02): match.
- c-03 (onsetAge) and c-04 (onsetString): not indexed, no match.
- c-05 (Period 2019-10 to 2020-02): starts before 2020-01-01, so it overlaps and matches.
- c-07 (2019-12-31 23:30 PST): before local midnight, match.
- c-08 (2020-01-01 01:00 PST): not before 2020-01-01, no match.
- Result: c-01, c-05, c-07.

**q2: `clinical-status=active,recurrence,relapse&onset-date=ge2020-01-01`**
- Status candidates: the six active resources plus c-02 (recurrence).
- Date filter: the target must end on or after 2020-01-01 00:00 PST.
- c-01 (2018) and c-02 (2019-07-01): no.
- c-03 and c-04: not indexed, no.
- c-05 (Period ending 2020-02): overlaps, match.
- c-07 (2019-12-31 23:30 PST): ends before the cutoff, no.
- c-08 (2020-01-01 01:00 PST): match.
- Result: c-05, c-08.

**q3: `onset-date:missing=true`**
- Resources with no indexed onset value: c-03 (onsetAge), c-04 (onsetString), c-11 (no onset).
- c-06 (`"2015"`) is a valid year-precision dateTime, so it is indexed and excluded.
- Result: c-03, c-04, c-11.

**q4: `clinical-status:not=active`**
- Excludes only the six resources with `active` in the condition-clinical system.
- Includes c-09 (no clinicalStatus) and c-10 (`Active` from the local system, which does not match the code `active`).
- Result: c-02, c-06, c-09, c-10, c-11.

**q5: `onset-date=2019`**
- Search range is [2019-01-01, 2020-01-01) PST.
- c-02 (2019-07-01): match.
- c-05 (Period overlapping 2019): match.
- c-07 (2019-12-31 23:30 PST): match.
- c-08 (2020-01-01 01:00 PST): outside the range.
- c-01, c-06, c-09, c-10: outside 2019.
- Result: c-02, c-05, c-07.
