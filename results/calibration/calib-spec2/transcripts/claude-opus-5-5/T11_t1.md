## Reply

**Key interpretation points**

- **Separate `_has` parameters are ANDed independently.** Each one can be satisfied by a different Observation. Only a composite parameter binds `code` and `value` to the same Observation.
- **`value-quantity=gt9` means value > 9.** This follows the R4 number semantics, where `gt100` means "greater than 100". No unit is given, so values in any unit match. The server's "range above starts at END" rule applies only to date prefixes, not quantities.
- **`gt9` matches every Observation except o-3 (7.0).** The matches are o-1 (9.5), o-2 (100), o-4 (250), o-5 (9.2), o-6 (10.1), o-7 (130), o-8 (11.0) and o-9 (95).
- **`status` is not filtered unless the query asks for it.** o-6 is `entered-in-error` but still matches searches with no `status` criterion.
- **o-8 points to Patient/pt-9, which is not on the server.** It can match direct Observation searches. It cannot make any Patient match, cannot satisfy the chained `patient.gender`, and its patient cannot be included.

**q1**
- The HbA1c (4548-4) condition matches pt-1 (o-1), pt-2 (o-3), pt-3 (o-5) and pt-5 (o-6).
- The value > 9 condition matches pt-1, pt-2 (via o-4), pt-3, pt-4 and pt-5.
- The intersection is pt-1, pt-2, pt-3 and pt-5.
- pt-2 matches only because two different Observations satisfy the two conditions.

**q2**
- The composite requires one Observation with HbA1c > 9. That gives o-1, o-5, o-6 and o-8.
- Mapped to patients on the server, the result is pt-1, pt-3 and pt-5.

**q3**
- Start from the q2 set.
- The separate `status=final` condition can be met by any final Observation for the patient. pt-1 (o-1), pt-3 (o-5) and pt-5 (o-7) all qualify.
- pt-5 stays in even though o-6 is entered-in-error.

**q4**
- The HbA1c > 9 Observations are o-1, o-5, o-6 and o-8.
- The female filter keeps o-1 (pt-1) and o-5 (pt-3).
- o-6 drops out because pt-5 is male. o-8 drops out because pt-9 cannot be resolved.

**q5**
- The matches are o-1, o-5, o-6 and o-8.
- The included patients are pt-1, pt-3 and pt-5. pt-9 does not exist, so nothing is included for o-8.

**q6**
- The matches are o-3 and o-4.
- The revinclude adds prov-2.

**q7**
- Patients with an HbA1c Observation are pt-1, pt-2, pt-3 and pt-5. The female ones are pt-1 and pt-3.
- `_revinclude=Provenance:target` adds prov-3, which targets pt-1.
- `_revinclude=Observation:patient` adds o-1 and o-2 (pt-1) and o-5 (pt-3).
