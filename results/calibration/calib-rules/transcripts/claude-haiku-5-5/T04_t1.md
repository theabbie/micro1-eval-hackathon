**Local-time conversions (America/Chicago)**
- May 2019 is CDT (UTC-5). Dec 2018 and Dec 31, 2019 are CST (UTC-6).
- imm-05: `2019-06-01T03:30Z` = 2019-05-31 22:30 CDT
- imm-06: `2018-12-31T23:00-06:00` = 2019-01-01T05:00Z = 2018-12-31 23:00 CST
- imm-08: `2020-01-01T05:30Z` = 2019-12-31 23:30 CST
- imm-03 and imm-10 use `occurrenceString`, which is not indexed by `date`, so they have no date value.

**q1: `date=2019-05`** (eq; whole month of May 2019, CDT)
- imm-01 (2019-05-14): inside. Match.
- imm-02 (2019): not inside.
- imm-04 (2019-05): inside. Match.
- imm-05 (May 31, 22:30 CDT): inside. Match.
- imm-06 (Dec 2018): no.
- imm-07 (2019-05-20): inside. Match. No status filter, so `not-done` is included.
- imm-08 (Dec 31, 2019): no.
- imm-09 (May 31, 23:59 CDT): inside. Match.

**q2: `date=ne2019-05`** (not eq; resources with a date value that are not wholly inside May 2019)
- Excluded as eq: imm-01, imm-04, imm-05, imm-07, imm-09.
- Matches: imm-02 (2019), imm-06 (Dec 2018), imm-08 (Dec 31, 2019).
- imm-03 and imm-10 have no value, so they never match `ne`.

**q3: `date=2019`** (eq; whole year 2019, local)
- imm-01, imm-02, imm-04, imm-05, imm-07, imm-08 (Dec 31, 2019 23:30 CST, still in 2019), imm-09 match.
- imm-06 (Dec 31, 2018 23:00 CST) is outside 2019.

**q4: `date=le2019-05-31&status=completed`** (le; some instant of T is before the end of S, where S = May 31 2019 local day, so the end is 2019-06-01T00:00 CDT)
- Status must be `completed`, which excludes imm-07.
- Starts before June 1, 2019: imm-01, imm-02, imm-04, imm-05, imm-06, imm-09 (23:59 CDT, before midnight).
- imm-08 (Dec 31, 2019) starts after the cutoff, so it does not match.
- imm-03 and imm-10 have no value.

**q5: `date:missing=true`**
- Only resources with no indexable date: imm-03 and imm-10 (both have `occurrenceString`).

**q6: `vaccine-code=…|03&date=gt2019-05-14`**

- imm-02 (all of 2019): yes.
- imm-03: no date value, so no.
