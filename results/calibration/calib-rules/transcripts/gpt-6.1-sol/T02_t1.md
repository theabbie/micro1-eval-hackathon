- **q2:** The bounds select intervals containing an instant at or after **05:00Z** and an instant before **06:00Z**. The day, month and collection period all satisfy both conditions.
- **q3:** `sa` requires the entire interval to start at or after **November 4, 05:00Z**.
- **q4:** `eb` requires every instant to precede **November 3, 04:00Z**.
- **q5:** `ne` returns the complement of q1 because every resource has an indexed date value.
