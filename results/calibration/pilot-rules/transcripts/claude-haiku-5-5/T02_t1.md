## Reply

**Setup.** America/New_York falls back at 02:00 EDT on 2024-11-03, so the local day 2024-11-03 is 25 hours long. In UTC it is S = [2024-11-03T04:00Z, 2024-11-04T05:00Z).

**Resource intervals (T), in UTC where needed:**
- k-01: 11-03 04:30Z (inside S)
- k-02: 11-03 03:30Z (before S)
- k-03: 11-04 04:30Z (inside S)
- k-04: 11-04 05:00Z (exactly the exclusive end of S)
- k-05: 11-03 05:30Z (inside S)
- k-06: 11-03 06:30Z (inside S)
- k-07: 11-03 06:30Z (12:00+05:30, inside S)
- k-08: whole local day, equal to S
- k-09: 23:45 EST on 11-03 = 11-04 04:45Z (inside S)
- k-10: whole month of 2024-11 (local), which extends beyond S
- k-11: period 11-03 02:00Z to 11:01Z, which overlaps S but is not contained in it
- k-12: 11-03 05:00Z (inside S)
- k-13: 11-03 06:00Z (inside S)

**q1, `date=2024-11-03` (eq; every instant of T inside S):** k-01, k-03, k-05, k-06, k-07, k-08, k-09, k-12, k-13. Excluded: k-02 and k-04 (outside), k-10 and k-11 (not contained).

**q2, `ge 05:00Z` AND `lt 06:00Z`:** The resource needs an instant at or after 2024-11-03T05:00Z and an instant before 2024-11-03T06:00Z.
- k-05 ✔ (05:30Z)
- k-08 ✔ (day spans 04:00Z to 05:00Z the next day, so it has instants on both sides)
- k-10 ✔ (month spans both bounds)
- k-11 ✔ (02:00Z to 11:01Z spans both bounds)
- k-12 ✔ (05:00Z)
- Excluded: k-01, k-02, k-03, k-04 (no instant ≥05:00Z before 06:00Z), k-06, k-07 and k-13 (single instant at or after 06:00Z), k-09 (after 06:00Z).

**q3, `sa` (every instant of T at or after the end of S = 11-04T05:00Z):** Only k-04 qualifies, since its single instant is exactly at the end. k-03 (04:30Z) and k-09 (04:45Z) are one hour early.

**q4, `eb` (every instant of T before the start of S = 11-03T04:00Z):** Only k-02 qualifies (03:30Z). k-08 starts at 04:00Z, so it does not qualify.

**q5, `ne` (has a value and is not eq):** Every resource has an effective value, so ne matches the complement of q1: k-02, k-04, k-10, k-11.
