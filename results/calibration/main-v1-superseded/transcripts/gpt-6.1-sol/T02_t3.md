## Reply

The local date `2024-11-03` spans **2024-11-03T04:00Z through 2024-11-04T05:00Z**, with the upper boundary excluded—a 25-hour day.

For date searches, `eq` requires the search range to contain the entire target range. Thus q1 excludes the month-wide k-10 and the collection period k-11. `ne` gives the complementary results here.

In q2, the bounds are `ge05:00Z` and `lt06:00Z`. Each constraint is evaluated independently, so the broader ranges of k-08, k-10 and k-11 satisfy both. k-13 starts exactly at the excluded upper boundary.

For q6, the search covers the minute beginning `06:30Z`; both k-06 and k-07 fall within it.
