## Reply

Date searches use Los Angeles time, so the cutoff is `2020-01-01T08:00:00Z`. Thus c-07 falls before 2020 locally, while c-08 falls after it.

- **q1:** c-05 matches `lt` because its Period extends before the cutoff, even though it also extends into 2020.
- **q2:** c-05 overlaps the search date and therefore matches `ge`; c-08 also matches.
- **q3:** Age and free-text onset values are not indexed by this date parameter. c-11 has no onset.
- **q4:** `:not=active` includes missing clinical status. The local code `Active` differs from `active` because matching is case-sensitive.
- **q5:** Equality requires the onset range to be contained within local calendar year 2019. c-05 extends beyond that year; c-07 is within it.
