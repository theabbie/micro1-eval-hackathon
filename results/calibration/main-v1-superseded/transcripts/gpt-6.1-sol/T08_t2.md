## Reply

February’s search range is February 1 at 00:00 EST through March 1 at 00:00 EST. March’s range ends April 1 at 00:00 EDT, accounting for the DST change.

An unprefixed date search requires the search range to contain the entire target range. Thus, periods crossing month boundaries, the year-wide value, and the open-ended period fail monthly equality searches. The combined `ge`/`le` query allows overlapping ranges, so those spanning March can match.

For q7, the minute-precision search range ends at April 1 at 00:00 EDT. `gt` matches targets extending above that boundary, including the year-wide value and open-ended period.
