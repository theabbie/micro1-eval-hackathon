## Reply

**Reference frame:** the local day 2024-11-03 in America/New_York runs from 04:00Z on Nov 3 (00:00 EDT) to 05:00Z on Nov 4 (00:00 EST), exclusive. That is 25 hours.

**Resources as UTC instants or ranges**
- k-01: 04:30Z Nov 3
- k-02: 03:30Z Nov 3
- k-03: 04:30Z Nov 4
- k-04: 05:00Z Nov 4
- k-05: 05:30Z Nov 3
- k-06: 06:30Z Nov 3
- k-07: 06:30Z Nov 3
- k-08: the whole local day, 04:00Z Nov 3 to 05:00Z Nov 4
- k-09: 23:45 local, which is 04:45Z Nov 4 (EST)
- k-10: all of November (Nov 1 04:00Z to Dec 1 05:00Z)
- k-11: a period from 02:00Z to 11:00Z on Nov 3
- k-12: 05:00Z Nov 3
- k-13: 06:00Z Nov 3

**q1 (`date=2024-11-03`, eq):** the target's range must lie entirely inside the search day.
- Inside the day: k-01, k-03, k-05, k-06, k-07, k-08, k-09, k-12, k-13.
- k-10 (month) and k-11 (period starting before the day) are not contained, so they are excluded.
- k-02 and k-04 fall outside the day.

**q2 (`ge2024-11-03T01:00-04:00` AND `lt2024-11-03T01:00-05:00`):** the two bounds are 05:00Z (ge) and 06:00Z (lt).
- ge needs the target to reach 05:00Z or later, or to overlap the search range.
- lt needs the target to start before 06:00Z.
- Passing both: k-05 (05:30Z), k-12 (05:00Z), k-08 (day range overlaps both), k-10 (month overlaps both), k-11 (02:00Z to 11:00Z overlaps both).
- k-13 (exactly 06:00Z) fails lt, because the range below ends at 06:00Z.
- k-01 and k-02 end before 05:00Z, so they fail ge.
- k-06, k-07, k-03, k-04 and k-09 start at or after 06:00Z, so they fail lt.

**q3 (`sa2024-11-03`):** the target must start at or after 05:00Z Nov 4. Only k-04 qualifies (exactly 05:00Z).

**q4 (`eb2024-11-03`):** the target must end before 04:00Z Nov 3. Only k-02 qualifies (03:30Z).

**q5 (`ne2024-11-03`):** the target is not fully contained in the search range, so it is the complement of q1: k-02, k-04, k-10, k-11.

**q6 (`date=2024-11-03T01:30-05:00`, eq):** this is 06:30Z with minute precision, so the range is [06:30:00Z, 06:31:00Z). Contained: k-06 and k-07. Note that k-05 at 01:30-04:00 is a different instant (05:30Z).
