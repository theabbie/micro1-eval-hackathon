**Setup.** July 2024 in America/New_York is EDT (UTC-4), so the July range is [2024-07-01T00:00-04:00, 2024-08-01T00:00-04:00). A Period with a missing start or end is treated as open on that side. A date-only or month-only value is its whole local range.

**q1: `date=2024-07` (eq).** The target's range must lie fully inside July.
- e-03 matches (15 July).
- e-05 matches. 02:00Z on 1 Aug is 22:00 EDT on 31 July, so it is inside July.
- e-06 matches. Its month-precision start and end both lie within July.
- e-01 and e-07 start in June.
- e-02 has an open end.
- e-04 ends on 1 Aug at 02:40 EDT.
- e-09 is on 30 June at 23:30 EDT.
- e-10 has an open start.
- e-08 has no period.

**q2: `date=ge2024-07-01&date=le2024-07-31` (AND).** The target must reach 1 July 00:00 or later, and must start before 1 Aug 00:00 EDT. Open ends count as reaching and open starts count as starting early.
- Matches: e-01, e-02, e-03, e-04, e-05, e-06, e-07 and e-10.
- e-07 ends 1 July 08:00 EDT, so it passes `ge`.
- e-10 has an open start and an end on 10 July, so it passes both.
- e-09 is excluded because it ends on 30 June at 23:50 EDT.
- e-08 is excluded because it has no period.

**q3: `class=…|IMP&date=sa2024-06-30`.** The IMP encounters are e-01, e-02 and e-08. `sa` requires a start at or after 1 July 00:00 EDT.
- e-02 matches (start 30 July).
- e-01 starts 28 June.
- e-08 has no date.

**q4: `date=gt2024-07-31`.** The target must have some part at or after 1 Aug 00:00 EDT.
- e-02 matches because its end is open.
- e-04 matches because it ends 1 Aug 02:40 EDT.
- e-05 ends 31 July at 22:30 EDT, so it does not match.
- e-06 ends at the end of July (exclusive bound), so it does not match.

**q5: `date=lt2024-07-01&class=AMB,OBSENC`.** The target must have some part before 1 July 00:00 EDT. The class candidates are AMB (e-03, e-05, e-06, e-09, e-10) and OBSENC (e-07).
- e-07 matches (starts 30 June at 23:30 EDT).
- e-09 matches (30 June at 23:30 EDT).
- e-10 matches because its start is open.
- e-06 starts exactly at 1 July 00:00, so it does not match.
- e-03 and e-05 are in July.

**q6: `date:missing=true`.** Only e-08 has no period. e-10 has a period with an end, so it has a value.
