# FHIR R4 Patient Search Results for T10

## String search rules applied

- **Default matching:** Case-insensitive and accent/diacritic-insensitive. The search value must match the start of the value. On this server, family names are also matched by their parts, split on spaces and hyphens.
- **`:contains`:** Case- and accent-insensitive. The value may appear anywhere within the whole element value.
- **`:exact`:** Case- and accent-sensitive. It must equal the whole element value.

## Query analysis

**q1 `name=munoz`**
- p-01 matches: "Muñoz" folds to "munoz".
- p-07 matches: its old name is "Munoz". `name` searches every HumanName, whatever its `use` or `period`.

**q2 `family=quinones`**
- p-02 matches. The family "Carreño Quiñones" splits into the parts "Carreño" and "Quiñones". The second part folds to "quinones".

**q4 `given:contains=eve`**

**q5 `given:exact=Eve`**

**q8 `identifier=system|000123`**
