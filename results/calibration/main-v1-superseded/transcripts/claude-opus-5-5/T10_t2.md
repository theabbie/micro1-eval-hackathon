## Per-query reasoning

Default (no-modifier) string matching in FHIR R4 is case-insensitive and accent-insensitive, and the field must equal or start with the search value. On this server, family names are also matched by their parts, split on spaces and hyphens.

**q1 `name=munoz`**
- p-01 matches: "Muñoz" normalises to "munoz".
- p-07 matches through its old name "Munoz". The `use` and `period` of a HumanName do not filter the search.

**q2 `family=quinones`**
- p-02 matches: "Carreño Quiñones" splits into "Carreño" and "Quiñones", and "Quiñones" normalises to "quinones".

**q3 `given=eve`** (starts-with)
- Matches: p-03 "Eve", p-04 "Evelyn", p-09 "EVE" and p-10 "Eve".
- No match: "Séverine" (p-05) and "Steve" (p-06) do not start with "eve". p-08 has "Eve" only as a family name, and `given` does not search family names.

**q4 `given:contains=eve`** (case- and accent-insensitive, anywhere in the value)
- Matches: p-03, p-04, p-09 and p-10 as in q3.
- Also matches: p-05, because "Séverine" normalises to "severine", which contains "eve". p-06 also matches, because "Steve" contains "eve".

**q5 `given:exact=Eve`** (case- and accent-sensitive, whole value)
- Matches: p-03 and p-10.
- No match: p-09 "EVE" differs in case.

**q6 `name=jon`** (checks family, given, prefix, suffix and text)
- Matches:
  - p-03: "Smith-Jones" splits into "Jones", which starts with "jon".
  - p-06: family name "Jones".
  - p-08: given name "Jon".
- No match:
  - p-05 "Johansson" starts with "joh", not "jon".
  - p-02's text starts with "Ana". The text value is not split into parts.

**q7 `family:exact=Smith`**
- Matches: p-10 only.
- No match: p-09 "SMITH" differs in case. p-03 "Smith-Jones" is not equal as a whole value, and parts are not used with `:exact`.

**q8 `identifier=hospital-mrn|000123`**
- Matches: p-01 only.
- No match: p-02's value is "123", which is a different token. p-03 has a different system.

**q9 `identifier=000123`** (any system)
- Matches: p-01, p-03, and p-06 through its DL identifier.

**q10 `identifier:of-type=v2-0203|MR|000123`**
- The type and the value must appear on the same identifier.
- Matches: p-01 and p-03.
- No match: p-06's "000123" is a DL identifier, and its MR identifier is "000126". p-10 has no type.

**q11 `name=dr&gender=male`**
- p-06 matches: its prefix "Dr." starts with "dr", and its gender is male.
- No other patient has a name part starting with "dr".
