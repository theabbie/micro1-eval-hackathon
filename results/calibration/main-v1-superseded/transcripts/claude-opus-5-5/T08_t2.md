- **q1 (eq Feb):** The target must lie fully inside February.
  - Matches: g-01, g-05, g-10, g-12, g-13.
  - g-09 fails because it spans into March.
- **q2 (eq Mar):** The target must lie fully inside March.
  - Matches: g-02, g-03, g-06, g-07, g-08, g-11, g-14.

- **q3 (ge 03-01 AND le 03-31):** Under this server's rules, the two prefixes reduce to:
  - ge: the target ends after 03-01 05:00Z.
  - le: the target starts before 04-01 04:00Z.

  - g-04 starts after 04:00Z, so it fails.

- **q5 (sa 03-31):** The target must lie fully at or after 04-01 04:00Z. Only g-04 qualifies.
- **q6 (ne Mar):** The target must not be fully contained in March. Every resource has a date value, so the result is the complement of q2.
- **q7 (gt …23:59-04:00):** The range above starts at 04-01 04:00Z, and the target must extend past that point.
