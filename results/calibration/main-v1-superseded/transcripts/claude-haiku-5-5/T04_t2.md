**Query analysis**

- **q1 `date=2019-05`** (eq, search range May 2019): eq requires the target range to lie fully inside the search range. imm-01, imm-04, imm-05, imm-07, imm-09 qualify. imm-02 (whole 2019) does not, since it is not contained in May. imm-03 and imm-10 have no indexed date.
- **q2 `date=ne2019-05`**: ne matches targets whose range is not fully contained in May 2019, and resources with no value are not matched. imm-02 (2019), imm-06 (2018), and imm-08 (2019-12-31) qualify.
- **q3 `date=2019`** (eq, search range 2019): all indexed values local to 2019 qualify. imm-06 (2018) is out. imm-08 is in because its local time is 2019-12-31 23:30.
- **q4 `date=le2019-05-31&status=completed`**: le matches targets at or before the start of the search range (2019-05-31 00:00) and any target overlapping the search range. Matches: imm-01 and imm-06 (before), imm-02 and imm-04 (overlap, since the year and month ranges cover 05-31), imm-05 and imm-09 (overlap on 05-31). imm-08 is after and excluded. imm-07 is removed by `status=completed`.
